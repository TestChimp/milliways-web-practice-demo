## Summary

Signed-in guests see the Milliways menu grouped into Main dishes and Drinks, open an item to choose a quantity (and a spice level for main dishes), and add it to their order.

## Acceptance criteria

- Menu shows both sections with names, descriptions and prices in ₭.
- Item dialog lets the guest change quantity before adding.
- Main dishes offer spice levels Mild, Medium, Hot and Supernova (Supernova adds ₭1.50 per portion); drinks have no spice option.
- The order bar shows item count and running total.
