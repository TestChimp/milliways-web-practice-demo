## Summary

A guest creates a Milliways account with an email and password, or signs in to an existing one, before browsing the menu and ordering.

## Acceptance criteria

- Sign-up with a new email creates the account and lands on the welcome screen.
- Sign-in with valid credentials lands on the welcome screen.
- Wrong credentials show an error and keep the user on the sign-in page.
- An email can only be registered once.
