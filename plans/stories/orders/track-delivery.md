## Summary

After placing an order the guest sees a delivery screen with the order status (polled from the API), the delivery method, the total and any savings.

## Acceptance criteria

- Delivery screen shows status 'Received' and the chosen method.
- Closing the screen clears the order and returns to the welcome screen.
